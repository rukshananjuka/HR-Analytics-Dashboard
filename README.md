Key Insights & Features

1. High-Level KPIs
Total Headcount: Managed a dataset of 1,470 total employees.

Attrition Tracking: Identified 237 total employee departures, resulting in an overall Attrition Rate of 16.12%.

Demographics: The active workforce stands at 1,233 employees with an average age of 37 years.

2. Attrition Breakdown & Deep Dives
Department-wise Analysis: Research & Development experiences the highest volume of attrition (56.12% of total attrition), followed closely by Sales (38.8%).

Education Field Impact: Employees from a Human Resources background exhibit the highest rate of attrition (26%), closely followed by Technical Degrees (24%).

Demographic Cross-Analysis: The dashboard includes a localized breakdown of attrition rates by both Gender and Age Groups (e.g., Under 25, 25–34, 35–44, etc.), helping HR partners identify exactly where retention risks are highest.

3. Employee Sentiment Mapping
Job Satisfaction Matrix: Built a cross-tabulated matrix evaluating satisfaction levels (scaled 1–4) across various job roles (e.g., Sales Executives, Laboratory Technicians, Research Scientists). This maps out exact satisfaction gaps corresponding to high-attrition roles.

Tech Stack & Tools Used
Business Intelligence: Microsoft Power BI Desktop

Data Transformations: Power Query (Data cleaning, age-banding categorization, and column normalization)

Design & Layout: Custom UI styling utilizing targeted color themes for high scannability, card visualizations, and interactive slicers (Education Field filters).

Technical Implementation Highlights
Age Banding: Created custom conditional columns (CF_age_band) to group employee data dynamically into actionable cohorts (e.g., Under 25, 25-34, etc.).

Advanced Charting: Utilized customized donut charts with dual-metric labels (showing both rates and proportions) to track complex categorical trends like gender-based attrition over time.
